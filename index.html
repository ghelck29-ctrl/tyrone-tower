Before your visit

A few helpful details.

How do I find out what is available?

Contact Blakemore Kearney at Vector Commercial Real Estate Services for current suites, pricing and lease terms. The listing link above is a useful starting point; confirm details directly before making plans.

What does full service include?

Rent covers utilities, building services and property expenses, so there is one predictable payment.

Can a suite be built out for medical use?

Most suites can. The leasing team will walk the space with you and scope the work.

Can I tour the building?

Yes, contact the leasing team to request a tour and coordinate a suitable time. A tour is not scheduled until the team confirms it.

Is there on-site parking?

Yes, the property has on-site parking. Ask the leasing team about allocation, reserved spaces and visitor arrangements for your proposed suite.

Who should existing tenants contact?

Refer to your lease or tenant communications for your property-management contact and service procedures. Leasing inquiries can be directed to Vector using the details below.
